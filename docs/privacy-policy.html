Twinkle Twist – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Twinkle Twist is a puzzle game for Android made by Filip Jönsson. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

What the game itself stores

Your progress (levels, stars, hints and settings) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us, and we do not run any servers. The game needs no account and does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details. Uninstalling the app, or choosing "Reset progress" in Settings, removes this data.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your approximate location,
  • information about how you interact with ads and the app,
  • diagnostic information such as crash logs and performance data.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, measure them, and prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → "Ad choices and privacy" in the game. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Read more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Rewarded ads

If you choose to watch a rewarded ad to get more hints, the same advertising rules as above apply. Watching rewarded ads is always optional.

Children

Twinkle Twist is intended for players aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Permissions

The app only uses internet access, which is needed to load ads. The game itself works without a network connection. Vibration feedback needs no special permission.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
