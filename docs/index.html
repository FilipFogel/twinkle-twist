Twinkle Twist

Turn the pieces until the whole string lights up.

A calm puzzle game about a tangled string of lights. Tap a piece to turn it a quarter turn. When every cable leads back to the power socket, every lamp comes on.

Play in your browser Get it on Google Play

Free, with no account. The browser version has no ads.

How it works

  1. Tap to turn

    Each tap turns a piece a quarter turn. Here the string is broken in the middle.

  2. Light every lamp

    Join the cables so power reaches all of them. When every lamp shines, the level is done.

  3. Use fewer moves

    The fewer taps you need, the more stars you earn. Stuck? A hint puts one piece right.

What is in the game

Levels that keep growing

The board starts at 3×3 and grows to 7×9. There is no last level.

New twists along the way

Screwed-down pieces, holes in the board, twins that turn together and edges that wrap around.

A daily string

One new puzzle every day, the same for everyone. Share your result and see who needs fewer moves.

Seven languages

English, Swedish, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Indonesian.

Your progress stays with you

Levels, stars and hints are saved on your own device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Snow at Christmas

From 22 November to 6 January it snows in the game.
