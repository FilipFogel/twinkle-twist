## Full description (max 4000)

First paragraph during the Christmas season:

Twinkle Twist is a cozy puzzle game about a tangled string of Christmas lights.

First paragraph the rest of the year:

Twinkle Twist is a calm puzzle game about a tangled string of lights.

The rest, all year:

The board is full of cable pieces lying every which way. Tap a piece and it turns a quarter turn. When every cable connects back to the power socket, every lamp lights up and the level is done. The fewer moves you use, the more stars you earn.

HOW IT PLAYS
• Tap a piece to turn it.
• Connect all the cables to the power socket.
• Light every lamp to finish the level.
• Press and hold a piece to pin it when you know it is right.
• Stuck? A hint puts one piece in place.

LEVELS THAT KEEP GROWING
The game starts on a small 3×3 board and grows step by step to 7×9. New twists arrive along the way:
• Screwed-down pieces that already face the right way
• Holes in the board that the string has to go around
• Twins: two pieces that turn together
• Boards where the edges wrap around

There is no last level and no timer. Take the time you want.

A NEW DAILY STRING EVERY DAY
Every day brings one new puzzle, the same for everyone. Keep your streak going, earn a hint, and share your result to see who needs fewer moves.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Free to play, with no purchases
• No account and no sign-in
• Your progress is saved on your phone
• The game itself works offline
• In English, Swedish, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Indonesian
• From 22 November to 6 January it snows in the game

Twinkle Twist is funded by ads: a short one now and then between levels, and an optional one if you want more hints.

You can also play in your browser: https://filipfogel.github.io/twinkle-twist/
